## What are you trying to do?

Describe the goal or task you are trying to accomplish.
Focus on *what* you want to achieve, not how it should be implemented.

## What problem are you running into?

Explain what is currently difficult, confusing, slow, or impossible.
If something is frustrating, describe why.

## What would a good solution look like to you?

In your own words, describe what success looks like.
This can be a behavior, outcome, or experience — not technical details.

## Who would this help?

- Just me
- My team
- Many users
- Not sure

(Optional) Describe who benefits and how often.

## How important is this?

- Nice to have
- Important
- Blocking my work

(Optional) Add any timing or urgency context.

## Additional context (optional)

Include anything else that may help:
- Screenshots
- Examples
- Links
- Current workarounds
